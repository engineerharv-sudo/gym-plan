Muscle plan

Upper / lower split, four days a week. Tap a circle when a set is done.

Rules that make it work

  • When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time.
  • Stop most sets with 1-3 reps left in the tank.
  • Rest 2-3 minutes after big lifts, 60-90 seconds after smaller ones.
  • Warm up with 5 minutes of light cardio and a lighter ramp-up set before your first heavy lift.
  • Eat enough protein, keep a small calorie surplus, and sleep 7-9 hours.
